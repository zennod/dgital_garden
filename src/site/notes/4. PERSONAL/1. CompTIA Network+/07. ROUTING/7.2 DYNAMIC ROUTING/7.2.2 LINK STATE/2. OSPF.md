- It stands for **OPEN SHORTEST PATH FIRST.**
- It's the most used ***IGP (INTERIOR GATEWAY PROTOCOL)*** used the world & the large companies also use this ***as their internal networks.***
- If ***using DRP (Dynamic Routing Protocol)*** it's possible that you are ***using OSPF.***
***
# ADVANTAGES 
- Its more ***FASTER & EFFICENT*** than RIPs.
- Also supports ***AUTHENTICATION***.
***
# DISADVANTAGES 
- ***OSPFs metrics is cost, which functions as an BANDWIDTH***; meaning that **the faster the bandwidth the lower will be the cost.**
	- **COST ∝ BANDWIDTH ∝ CONNECTION INTERFACE**

- If OSPF is used instead of RIP, **it has to go through more routers (HOP) to get to the destination networks**

	- *The more HOPs doesn't mean **slower**.* It depends on the interface & the connection medium too.
	- ***For ex*** - If the ***link speed is 1.554 mbps*** & the routers are connected through the **FIBRE** & the packet ***undergo 3 HOPs***, it reach much **QUICKER** due to the ***medium & the link speed.***
***
# IMPROVEMENTS.
1. OSPF is ***capable of sending out packets to other OSPF routers***.
2. **After 2 adjacent routers form a Neighborship/Relationship through packet, they exchange information about Routers & Networks.**
3. ==The information is exchanged by the packets called ***LINK STATE ADVERTISEMENT (LSA)***.==
4. The LSAs are **sourced out by each router**, meaning that it is ***generated by each router***; 
	- And **flooded from router to router** through OSPF area, meaning **LSAs are send to each router**.
5. All the routers communicate & individually decide their own ***OPTIMAL ROUTES & CONVERGENCE*** (Meeting Pt.)
6. If the routers goes down, ***the OSPF recomputes the new routes with the stored LSAs***.
***
# FUNCTIONS- 
1. The OSPF **uses AREAS (LANs, MANs, WANs)** to help r*outers re-routes their traffic, when the link's drop*.
2. When interconnected with multiple AREAS (LAN + WAN) - **The control area is known as BACK - BONE**.
3. The Backbone has an **AREA ID of 0 (zero) OR 0.0.0.0;**
	- ***All the traffic has to pass through the BACKBONE.***
4. It also ***resolves the LINK FAILURES. & Creates a pt. of CONVERGENCE***, making it the choice for the large companies for DRP.
5. ***OSPF Version 2*** is used for **IPv4** &  ***OSPF Version 3** for **IPv6.**

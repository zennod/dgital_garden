#http 
![67fb2d22a13985c70f7c3584501607ec.png](/img/user/_resources/67fb2d22a13985c70f7c3584501607ec.png)

The Image is a sample of ***HTTP header***, is the response from ***WEB SERVER*** to the ***CLIENT***

- The ***"HTTP/1.1"*** indicates the ***version*** of the ***HTTP Protocol***. 
- The ***"200 OK"*** indicates that the ***request was successful***.
- The Red blank is the ***separator*** between the ***BODY & HEADER***

#AWS #devops 

> [!note] Definition
> Devops is a process of improving the application **DELIVERY** by ensuring **PROPER AUTOMATION** with **CODE QUALITY** maintenance & ensuring **CONTINUOUS MONITORING** & **CONTINUOUS TESTING.**

- In *DevOps*, 
	- DEV contains creating a **DOCKER FILE, IMAGE & CONTAINER** of the *APPLICATION* made.
	- OPS contains **DOWNLOADING OF IMAGE, RUN THE IMAGE & OPERATE THE IMAGE**
***

# WHY DEVOPS - 
![Pasted image 20240404215829.png](/img/user/_resources/Pasted%20image%2020240404215829.png)
- Since many roles were involved, it took more time to deploy.
- There was manual work which caused delay in deployment.
- To automate this entire process the **DEVOPS** was introduced for faster **DEPLOYMENT**.
